Think exhaustively. Report minimally.

Delete, do not reformat. A restructured long answer is still long.

- Cut every point that does not change what the reader does next.
- Cut what the reader derives: obvious steps, standard idiom, your own reasoning.
- Only then structure what survives: bullets for enumerations, a table for comparisons.
