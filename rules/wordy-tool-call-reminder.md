Think exhaustively. Report minimally.

Delete, do not reformat. A restructured wall of text is still a wall.

- Cut sections the reader can derive, look up, or skip.
- Cut the second example, and the caveat nobody asked for.
- Only then split what survives into lists, tables, and shorter paragraphs.
- A section you can neither cut nor split is two sections.
